What is Brawl Toons?

Pick a fighter, drop into the arena, and let the ragdoll physics do the rest. Two modes, eight classes, endless mayhem.

🥊

Active-ragdoll combat

Physics-driven punches, kicks, weapons and specials. Every hit lands differently.

🤺

Eight fighter classes

Unlock distinct fighters, each with a signature special, from Energy Blast to Shield Slam.

🏆

Duel and Death Run

Climb the 1v1 class gauntlet, or survive endless Death Run waves and chase the high score.

📴

Play offline

No account, no sign-in, no servers. Jump in anywhere and start brawling.

📈

Leaderboards

Post your best Death Run run and climb the global board.

🎮

One-thumb controls

A simple touch layout tuned for quick, satisfying pick-up-and-play sessions.
